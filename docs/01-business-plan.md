# Business plan — Snow Village (v1.0, 2026-11-09)

## Executive thesis
Friends co-create a miniature winter village. Each friend claims one of 8 fixed cottages, chooses a style, nickname, and optionally one photo/message. New participants light up cottages; the completed village can be shared as a **photo-free** visual card. Invitees may start their own village. The growth hypothesis—not a proven outcome—is that collaborative visual progress motivates invitations and remixes.

## Positioning
Not a photo album, not a message tree, not a Secret Santa tool. A small shared **place** that visualizes belonging. Main differentiation is spatial co-creation with a highly polished result, not generic anonymous messages or a web invite link.

## Customer
Primary experiment: Korean friend groups of 3–8 (late teens / university students). Secondary: friends/couples aged 25–34; families; school clubs. These segments are hypotheses, not validated personas.

## Value proposition
Creator: make a visually charming group keepsake without editing software. Invitee: contribute a cottage in ~30 seconds with no app installation. Group: watch the village fill and export a keepsake.

## Product scope
Free MVP: 1 winter theme, 8 slots, 3 house styles, nickname, optional cropped photo and <=60-character message, house-frame modal, link invite, village completion effect, photo-free share image, owner moderation/deletion. Exclude chat, likes, in-app payments, AI generation, free placement and complex avatar movement.

## Acquisition / growth
- Creator shares link to fill houses.
- Invitee claims a house and sees it light up.
- Participant can create a new village with a separate group.
- Result card can be shared independently.
- Do not equate a share-button tap with successful delivery; instrument link opens and downstream conversions.

## Experiment funnel and targets (NOT market benchmarks)
Landing→create >=20%; invite-open→house-create >=40%; average participants/village >=4; participant→new-village >=20%; finished-village share >=30%. Under simple assumptions, 6 invites ×40% participation ×20% new-village conversion = 0.48 new villages per creator; this is **below** self-sustaining reproduction and requires additional discovery or improved conversion.

## Financial hypotheses
Initial cash budget target <=KRW 300,000 excluding labor. Hosting, storage, moderation and bandwidth must be measured. Optional future premium themes KRW 1,900/2,900/4,900 are price-test ideas, not validated willingness to pay. 2026 priority is retention and organic reproduction, not revenue.

## Milestones
Nov 10–13 concept/prototype tests; Nov 14–20 functional build; Nov 21–25 privacy/sharing; Nov 26–29 private beta; Dec 1 **aspirational** public MVP. Slip date if privacy, moderation or critical flows fail.

## Stop/pivot
Go: participation >=40%, new-village creation >=20%, repeated unsolicited sharing in >=30 independent groups. Pivot: people join but do not create new villages; investigate single-player shareable artifact. Stop: participation <15% after UX remediation. These are management thresholds, not statistically conclusive evidence.

## Risks
Cold-start; users reluctant to upload photos; Christmas seasonality; perceived similarity to tree-decoration apps; asset workload; inappropriate images; lost anonymous ownership; bandwidth costs; insufficient self-propagation. Mitigate via optional photo, original asset system, invite-first UX, server-side privacy, small scope and real group testing.

## Post-season
Only after validated traction, test birthday, graduation, spring and summer village themes. Do not build before MVP.
