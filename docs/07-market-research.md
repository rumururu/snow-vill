# Market positioning & research notes

## Observed competitive categories (qualitative)
- Anonymous Christmas message trees: Deco My Tree / '내 트리를 꾸며줘' — strong precedent for seasonal sharing; generic tree+message clone is weak differentiation.
- Secret Santa: Elfster, DrawNames, GiftList — invitations, wishlists and anonymous matching already common.
- Private friends photo sharing: Locket, Yope, Setlog — small-group lightweight content and repeat interactions.
- Event invites: Partiful — invitation is part of the core product, not a decorative share button.
- General photo albums: Google Photos — Snow Village must be a visual co-created place, not merely storage.

## Strategy inference
Avoid generic anonymous messages, wishlists, Santa videos, AI filters and party RSVPs. Focus on 8-friend collaborative scene where one photo or default illustration lights up one house. A shareable completed artifact and invite-to-create loop are product hypotheses requiring real tests.

## Research limitations
Prior discussion included approximate download/MAU reports from various dates; these are not independently audited here and should not be treated as a market-sizing model. No verified Korean app-store rank, 30-day downloads, acquisition cost, retention, willingness to pay, or competitor feature census has been established. Research must be refreshed before investment decisions.
